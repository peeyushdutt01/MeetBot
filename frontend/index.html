04 / GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
you might ask.

Can I use a recording from a past meeting?+

Yes. Upload a supported audio recording and MeetBot can turn it into a report with a transcript and follow-ups.

Can MeetBot join a live meeting?+

Yes. You can invite MeetBot to a meeting in progress or schedule it ahead of time.

Where do I find the next steps?+

Action items are collected in your workspace. Mark the important ones to keep them on your dashboard.
